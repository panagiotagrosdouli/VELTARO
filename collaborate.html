VELTARO is a concept-stage mobility research initiative exploring road-and-air transportation. We are seeking conversations with engineers, researchers, operators and prospective users to understand real-world constraints before making product commitments.

01 / Dual mode

VELTARO V1

Exploratory mass, energy, safety and road/aviation feasibility questions. Both eVTOL and runway-based folding-wing approaches remain under study.

02 / Passenger missions

VELTARO AIR

Early discovery of passenger needs, mission definitions, airspace constraints and potential certification pathways.

03 / Delivery research

SKYCOURIER

Interviews on constrained delivery use cases, operations, risks and economics—without implying operating permits.

04 / Research services

VELTARO LABS

Testing demand for non-regulated desk-based research briefs and feasibility syntheses. No commercial service or technical certification is offered at this stage.

Who we would like to hear from

Potential users, logistics operators, university researchers, engineers, qualified regulatory specialists and organizations willing to share non-confidential perspectives.
