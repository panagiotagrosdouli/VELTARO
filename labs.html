Potential scope

A first research deliverable.

Subject to competence, scope agreement and any necessary professional review, a preliminary desk study could cover:

01 / Define

Problem and mission brief

Translate a stakeholder's mobility question into a documented use case, design assumptions and constraints.

02 / Examine

High-level feasibility

Review preliminary mass, energy, system dependencies and regulatory questions using source-traceable methods.

03 / Deliver

Evidence and next steps

Provide an assumptions register, uncertainty map and prioritized research recommendations—not a certified design.

How to explore collaboration

Start with the question.

  1. Describe your organization, the practical problem and the research question.
  2. Discuss scope, confidentiality, professional qualifications and whether any work is legally permitted.
  3. If suitable, agree on deliverables, pricing, timeline and written terms before any paid work begins.

No guaranteed acceptance, response time or paid service availability is implied.
