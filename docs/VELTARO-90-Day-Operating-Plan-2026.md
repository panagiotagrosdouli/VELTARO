# VELTARO — 90-day execution and budget v0.1
**Date:** 2026-10-18 | **Status:** Planning assumptions only (EUR). No expenses committed.

## First 30 days
- Week 1: portfolio briefs, customer hypotheses, founder operating location and team skills, branding/IP check.
- Week 2: interviews and conceptual mass/energy estimates; identify applicable road/aviation/drone rules.
- Week 3: three-country jurisdiction memo with professional quotes; basic financial model and website privacy audit.
- Week 4: independent technical review, evidence-based portfolio decision, go/no-go plan for next 60 days.

## Days 31–60
- V1 two architectures reviewed on mass, mission energy, road dimensions, failure modes.
- AIR: defined passenger/customer mission and initial requirements.
- SKYCOURIER: 2 concrete permitted use cases; unit economics including operators, insurance, maintenance, regulations.
- LABS: one demonstrable engineering report and outbound validation with potential customers.
- Investor material must label all assumptions, unsupported market sizing and early-stage status.

## Days 61–90
- Decision gate for the primary activity using documented weighted score.
- Decide incorporation country after tax and legal advisor review.
- Build a 12-month monthly cash forecast; identify team gaps and financing options.
- Prepare only the selected workstream for further engineering investment.

## KPIs / evidence gates
- 12 logged interviews (buyers, operators, technical experts), segmented by workstream.
- Four written one-page theses with clear customer and constraints.
- V1 first-order eVTOL vs fixed-wing technical comparison with explicit assumptions.
- Written 3-jurisdiction decision memo.
- Three independent risks per workstream with mitigations.
- One chosen workstream and documented 90-day decision.

## Controls
Use monthly burn tracking and decision log. Require approval before committing funds, collecting sensitive user data, starting hardware construction/testing, or publicly asserting performance/certification.
