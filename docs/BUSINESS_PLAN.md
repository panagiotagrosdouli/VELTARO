# VELTARO — venture hypothesis and validation plan

**Stage:** Concept exploration. No existing sales, fundraising round, confirmed partners, valuation, manufacturing line or market share is asserted.

## Vision
Explore whether luxury road-and-air mobility can provide practical value to narrowly defined customers while meeting strict safety and regulatory requirements.

## Customer research
- Interview 20–30 prospective customers/operators across relevant use cases; capture trip lengths, willingness-to-pay and operating constraints.
- Map door-to-door journey times including weather, takeoff access, transfer/parking and charging.
- Benchmark premium road travel, rotorcraft, general aviation and proposed eVTOL mobility.

## Business model hypotheses
1. Premium personal vehicle: very high certification, insurance, training, maintenance and after-sales burden.
2. Strategic demonstrator/technology IP: potential subsystem partnerships; IP position not yet validated.
3. Fleet/operator pathway: requires commercial operator compliance and verified unit economics.

## Costs to estimate
Engineering labor; prototypes and test rigs; insurance; certification; flight testing; suppliers; industrialization; infrastructure; customer support. No speculative financial projections are presented as fact.

## Stage-gated capital allocation
Feasibility research → architecture selection → demonstrators → integrated prototype → verification and approvals. Budget each gate from supplier quotes and test plans before publishing fundraising claims.

## Critical risks
Battery specific energy and peak power, weight growth, architecture complexity, certification path, public acceptance, noise, infrastructure access, runway, supply chain and safety liability.

## Go/no-go criteria
Proceed only when quantified mission advantage, plausible physical feasibility, manageable regulatory requirements, and financing commitments justify the next stage.

## Communication standards
Never imply working prototypes, certified operations, funded rounds or secured partnerships without evidence. Separate illustrations from product photos; publish sources for numerical claims.
