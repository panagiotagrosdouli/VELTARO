# VELTARO V1 — Product Requirements v0.1

**Date:** 2026-10-16  
**Status:** Concept / research only  
**Architecture decision:** OPEN — parallel evaluation of eVTOL and folding fixed-wing runway takeoff  
**Owner:** Founder, VELTARO AUTOMOBILI

## Mission
Explore a genuine premium road-legal vehicle capable of controlled flight from authorized operating locations. The ambition is not yet a validated product design.

## Stakeholder requirements
| ID | Requirement | Priority | Verification route |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1-01 | Operate as a legally approved road vehicle in target jurisdictions | Must | Regulatory requirements mapping, later type-approval evidence |
| V1-02 | Provide controlled powered flight | Must | Conceptual simulation, later testing and certification |
| V1-03 | Manage hazards to occupants, bystanders and other airspace users | Must | Hazard assessment, risk controls, later validation |
| V1-04 | Separate road and flight modes with clear interlocks | Must | Functional architecture, failure analysis |
| V1-05 | Have a plausible applicable road and aircraft approval route | Must | Expert regulatory review |
| V1-06 | Manage credible loss-of-power, propulsion, control or sensor failure cases | Must | System safety analysis |
| V1-07 | Deliver premium ergonomics and distinctive design | Should | Design review, user interviews |
| V1-08 | Prefer electric flight propulsion where technically feasible | Goal | Trade-off study |
| V1-09 | Define pilot/operator qualifications and interface | Must | Human-factors and regulatory study |

## Concept candidates
**A: Electric VTOL.** No runway, but substantial hover power, mass, thermal management, rotor protection, noise and complex flight controls. Authorized takeoff/landing sites and airspace still required.

**B: Folding fixed-wing runway vehicle.** Requires runway, wing stowage and structural interlocks; potentially better cruise efficiency and more familiar flight physics, but road dimensions, crashworthiness and conversion add weight and complexity.

**Do not select a winner before a comparable evidence-based trade study.**

## Initial target assumptions (unvalidated)
- Mission: personal point-to-point mobility, with road travel to/from approved aviation sites.
- Occupants: investigate 1–2 people.
- Electric propulsion: preferred concept, not a non-negotiable commitment.
- Takeoff: open; VTOL and runway configurations studied in parallel.
- Flight range, speed, takeoff mass, price, dimensions, availability and dates: TBD by analysis.

## Comparable architecture trade study
Evaluate both on: (1) total mass budget including road crash structures; (2) installed power, energy and flight reserves; (3) takeoff/landing area and infrastructure; (4) aerodynamic and propulsion feasibility; (5) safety and emergency options; (6) road and aviation regulatory complexity; (7) noise/environmental impact; (8) manufacturing and maintenance complexity; (9) cost, available expertise and resources.

Record evidence, uncertainty and assumptions for each score. Avoid numerical ranking until requirements and scoring weights are agreed.

## Verification stages
1. User and mission requirements interview (founder + representative prospective users).
2. Mission profile, operational restrictions and regulatory mapping.
3. First-order energy and mass budgets for BOTH architectures.
4. Pre-concept hazard analysis and stakeholder review.
5. External review by qualified aerospace/automotive engineers.
6. Gate decision: continue parallel study, select a direction, revise mission, or stop.

## Immediate deliverables — first 4 weeks
- Week 1: one-page mission definition and requirements baseline.
- Week 2: first-order mass/energy assumption sheet for each concept.
- Week 3: feasibility and risk matrix with evidence links.
- Week 4: independent review and decision memo.

## Non-claims
No manufactured prototype, engineering validation, range, speed, roadmap completion, aircraft certification, road approval, funding or commercial availability is asserted. This document is a proposal for research, not an engineering certification artifact.
