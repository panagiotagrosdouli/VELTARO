# VELTARO V1 — Comparable battery and power evidence, rev 0
**19 October 2026 | Public research only | Not V1 specifications**

## Verified source starting points
1. NASA Glenn, *Battery Cell-to-Pack Scaling Trends for Electric Aircraft*, 2021: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20210017488 . Research reports that cell-to-pack scaling varies with architecture, chemistry, thermal, structural and safety provisions. Do not substitute cell Wh/kg for installed usable pack Wh/kg.
2. NASA-funded design research *NASA concept vehicles and the engineering of advanced air mobility aircraft*: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/aeronautical-journal/article/nasa-concept-vehicles-and-the-engineering-of-advanced-air-mobility-aircraft/AA7E668D759491B1889299819A2F2715 . Its 400 Wh/kg **installed usable pack** value is a DESIGN ASSUMPTION in concept exploration, not verified V1 battery capability; source explored lower assumptions.
3. EASA SC E-19 electric/hybrid propulsion: https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/product-certification-consultations/electrichybrid-propulsion-system . Regulatory pathway context, not test data or V1 approval.
