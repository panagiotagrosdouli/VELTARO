# VELTARO — Jurisdiction decision framework v0.1
**Date:** 2026-10-18 | **Decision:** OPEN. This is not legal or tax advice.

## Candidate screen
| Country | Potential advantages | Questions needing written local advice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Greece | Greek/e-YMS incorporation route; proximity if core people and operations are in Greece | Founder tax residence, effective management, aviation/drone work locations, payroll, R&D incentives, banking |
| Cyprus | EU jurisdiction; company incorporation via Registrar and legal filings | Substance, registered office, directors, tax residency, annual compliance, lawyer fees, actual activity location |
| Estonia | Remote administration via e-Residency; online incorporation | E-residency is not tax residency; permanent establishment/effective management elsewhere, contact person, aviation operational presence |

## Weighted evaluation (working assumptions)
Tax nexus and legal substance 30%; technical ecosystem and flight-test access 20%; access to team and investors 20%; compliance/operating costs 15%; administration/banking 15%. Each criterion 1–5 only after adviser evidence; no recommended winner yet.

## Required questions
1. Where will founders live and manage the company?
2. Where will engineers and future physical testing operate?
3. What investor jurisdictions and grant eligibility matter?
4. Which IP holder, founders' equity, vesting and invention assignment arrangements are appropriate?
5. What are the total 1st-year and recurring costs, including accounting/payroll/legal?
6. What VAT, payroll, permanent establishment, corporate tax and reporting consequences arise from actual operations?

## Decision rule
Obtain written comparison from qualified advisors in shortlisted countries. Select only after ranking **real activity location and tax substance first**, not headline incorporation fee or tax rate. Company formation is a separate action requiring founder approval.
