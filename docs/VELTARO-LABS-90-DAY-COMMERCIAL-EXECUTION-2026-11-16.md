## First market wedge (testable hypothesis)
- **Ideal customer profile (ICP):** EU/Greek early-stage mobility hardware/software teams or small industrial R&D teams planning a mobility idea, with a named technical decision-maker and a small discretionary research budget. Secondary candidates: applied research teams and innovation units; institutional contacts are not presumed to be buyers.
- **Problem:** Before committing engineering budget, teams need a short source-backed analysis of competing options, assumptions, uncertainties and next tests. This must be tested in interviews, not claimed as established demand.
- **Proposed entry offer:** **VELTARO LABS — Mobility Evidence Brief**, a bounded, non-regulated, desk-based decision-support report. Proposed 6–10 pages plus (1) source/evidence register, (2) assumptions and limitations, (3) comparison table or simple reproducible analysis where competent, (4) prioritized next research steps and (5) optional 30-minute review call.
- **Scope excluded:** engineering sign-off, safety certification, regulatory opinions, commissioned airworthiness findings, designs for manufacture, prototype testing, predictions of real V1 performance, client confidential data through public tools.
- **Pricing experiment, NOT a public quote:** Present two different fixed-scope offers after buyer interviews: a narrower literature/evidence brief **€450–€750** and a more involved multidisciplinary desk review **€1,200–€2,000** (amounts before any applicable VAT). Validate cost, qualifications, comparables, buyer budget and legal terms before offering paid work. For any review outside team competence, involve and budget for a qualified independent specialist or decline the scope.
- **Competition to test:** customer's own engineers, internal search, universities, existing consultants and specialized software. Do not assert VELTARO is better before comparison.

### Pipeline stage definitions
Researched -> outreach authorized -> contacted -> consented conversation -> evidenced paid problem -> buyer asks for scope -> proposal issued -> signed contract and legal invoicing readiness -> delivered and accepted -> paid -> repeat referral.
- Never count a non-binding form submission as revenue.
- A qualified interview needs a relevant user/budget owner and a concrete recent pain, not merely a polite compliment.
- Record stage transitions with dates and source evidence in a **private** CRM; public CSV is a schema/template only.
- Booked revenue = signed contract only; recognized revenue and cash received tracked separately with accountant guidance. Track delivery hours and external reviewer costs.

## Legal, privacy, IP and capability gates — mandatory, owned actions
1. **Identity and control:** Founder confirms legal form, jurisdiction, registered entity status, name and IP chain; do not claim incorporation without registry proof. Greece is only a working candidate until operating/tax nexus is reviewed.
2. **Paid trading:** Licensed local accountant/lawyer verifies legal ability to contract/invoice, VAT, accounting requirements, appropriate KAD/activity and professional restrictions BEFORE payment or paid pilot. Entity establishment is not equivalent to engineering licensure.
3. **Customer safety and quality:** Scope and qualifications checked; independent specialist sign-off for topics outside capacity. Bounded non-regulated research only until authorized. Use a reviewed customer contract, liability/limitations and IP/acceptance terms.
4. **Data:** Founder confirms GDPR controller/processor, lawful processing, retention and deletion; verify FormSubmit mailbox delivery, obtain permission before case-study publication. No passwords, raw client content, contact names, emails or lead details in public GitHub.
5. **Brand:** Legal trademark clearance (EUIPO and relevant markets), domains and photo licenses; branded website does not confer ownership of the mark.
6. **V1:** Both VTOL and folding-wing concepts remain HOLD; independent aerospace/automotive/regulatory evidence and legally permitted tests are prerequisites for any upgrade.

## Business math — illustrative only, not sales forecasts
90-day booking scenarios for feasibility, using prices to test rather than promise:
- Downside: **0 pilots × €900 = €0**.
- Lean validation: **1 pilot × €900 = €900**.
- Stronger signal: **2 pilots × €1,500 = €3,000**.
- Upside stress test: **4 pilots × €1,500 = €6,000** (NOT the baseline KPI).
The scenarios are gross nominal contract values before VAT, expenses, taxes, subcontractors and delivery time; **they are not profit**. Revenue must not be claimed until actually earned under applicable accounting rules. Update docs/mission/financial-model.xlsx with verified quotes and actuals only after review, preserving assumptions separately.

Track: cash collected, pipeline conversion with small-sample caution, direct delivery costs, actual founder hours, gross margin after relevant variable costs, bank balance, monthly burn and runway. Do not infer scalability from one custom study.
