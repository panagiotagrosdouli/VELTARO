## Engineering data still missing
- **Track A, folding wing / runway:** actual takeoff/climb/cruise/landing demand, structure/road mass and installed pack data for the selected configuration.
- **Track B, VTOL:** hover/transition/cruise loads; discharge-power duration limits; rotor/airframe/motor efficiency; pack thermal and road protection burden.
- **Both:** measured pack-level usable energy density, continuous and peak kW at specified SOC/temperature and duration, degradation, structural/cooling mass, traceable test methodology and independent review.

## Research policy
These are **references to existing public research, not verified subsystem specifications for V1**. The paper's 400 Wh/kg number is a *design assumption* that must never become a V1 range, weight or feasibility claim. No verified component quotes, tested pack hardware, independently validated vehicle models or product approvals exist in this register.

### Next permitted implementation
Create an external-source evidence input for each requested physical quantity, with citation, engineering boundary, operating condition, uncertainty and reviewer. Reject claims that mix nominal cell figures and usable installed pack figures. No supplier/partner communication, purchases or imagery changes.
