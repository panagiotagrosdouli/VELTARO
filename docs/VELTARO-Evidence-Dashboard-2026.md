## Decision gates
**Gate A — Commercial:** Is there a repeated, concrete need from at least two independent relevant interviews? If not, adjust or stop; interest does not equal an order.

**Gate B — Engineering:** Is the mission bounded and is there traceable evidence for each mass, energy and reserve assumption? If not, postpone numeric feasibility claims.

**Gate C — Publication:** Have actual mobile/desktop, form-delivery, privacy and image-rights checks been completed? If not, do not assert full launch readiness.

**Gate D — Cash:** Is the expenditure specifically funded and separately approved? If not, do not commit funds.

## How to update
Record **only verified actions**. Keep personal interview contact details off public GitHub. Date any change and link to an appropriately shareable evidence artifact.
