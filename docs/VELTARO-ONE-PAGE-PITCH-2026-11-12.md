# VELTARO | One-page founder pitch — concept-stage draft
*12 November 2026. Founder review required; no customer traction or certification claims.*

## Vision
Explore the future of premium road-and-air mobility while building a foundation of rigorous, traceable early-stage systems research.

## The problem
Ambitious mobility concepts often face a gap between visual ambition and evidence-based assessment of safety, energy, mass, regulatory constraints and a real customer use case.

## Initial business experiment
**VELTARO LABS** could offer a narrowly scoped preliminary feasibility brief to innovation or engineering teams. This is a proposed service, **not an established product or validated market**. A qualified professional must review all work where regulated engineering expertise or approvals are required.

## What we can show today
- Public research website and explanatory concepts.
- Preliminary V1 architecture trade-offs, mass/energy research tools and uncertainty/evidence registers.
- Test scripts and CI for code/data consistency.
These do **not** demonstrate real-world vehicle feasibility.

## Long-term research directions
**V1** — road-and-air concept; **AIR** — compact aviation concept; **SKYCOURIER** — potential freight drone applications. Each remains research-stage, subject to independent engineering and regulatory evidence.

## Current unknowns (explicit)
Paying customer and price, incorporation and IP status, validated system performance, regulatory pathway, physical prototype, team capacity, funding and supplier agreements.

## What we seek first
1. Structured conversations with possible industry customers.
2. Independent aerospace/automotive and regulatory review.
3. A tightly scoped first research collaboration or pilot, only if technically and legally supportable.
4. Source-grounded cost estimates for the next engineering gate.
