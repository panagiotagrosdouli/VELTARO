# VELTARO V1 — Two parallel architecture workstreams
**19 October 2026 | Concept research only | Both tracks remain active**

## Track A — Foldable-wing roadable aircraft
- **A1, operational route:** Define a credible origin → road segment → lawful departure runway → aviation route → lawful arrival runway → road segment. Record access constraints and operating jurisdiction.
- **A2, configuration and structure:** Explicit wing span in road/flight configurations only after credible dimensional evidence; define folding joints, locks, structure, crashworthiness, occupant protection and mass accounting boundaries.
- **A3, power/energy:** Independently define taxi, takeoff, climb, cruise, diversion/reserve and landing phases; pack-level capability, total mass feedback, wind/weather assumptions and thermal demands.
- **A4, safety:** Identify lock failure, uncommanded movement, road/flight interlock failure, aborted takeoff, occupant egress and emergency landing hazards.

## Track B — Powered-lift / VTOL roadable vehicle
- **B1, operational route:** Define a credible origin → road segment → lawful VTOL departure facility → aviation route → lawful arrival facility → road segment; study actual facility and airspace constraints.
- **B2, power/energy:** Explicitly separate hover, takeoff, climb, transition, cruise, descent, landing, contingency and reserve; include discharge limits, battery thermal conditions, rotor configuration and redundancy assumptions.
- **B3, integrated mass:** Study propulsor protection on roads, road crashworthiness, structural load paths, motor/controller count, noise shielding, thermal management and occupant/pedestrian separation.
- **B4, safety:** Study power loss during hover, transition control, rotor/propulsor exposure, lost-link and emergency landing/evacuation scenarios. Do not assume survivability or redundancy.

## Shared evidence gates
Both tracks need a jurisdiction-specific legal mission, cited engineering boundaries and independently reviewed safety approach. Until then, all subsystem masses, flight ranges, speeds, costs and readiness claims are **TBD**. No design can be selected on aesthetic grounds.

The research paths are **parallel, not mutually exclusive for study**, but they are not two committed production programs. Capital remains €0; no physical prototypes, partner outreach, purchasing or vehicle operation are authorized.

## Review definition
Each task is marked OPEN until it has: a source, date, applicability statement, explicit assumption range, owner/reviewer, uncertainty, and documented safety implications. Publishing numbers requires separate review.
