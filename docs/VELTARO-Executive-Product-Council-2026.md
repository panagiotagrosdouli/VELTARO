| SKYCOURIER | Parcel delivery operators | Mission-specific aerial delivery potential | 3 operator interviews and risk-aware unit-economics draft | Restrictions and real operating costs represented |

VELTARO LABS remains a **cross-cutting research/services validation track**, not a fifth vehicle product or an active certified consultancy.

## Executive council decision protocol
1. **CPO / CCO:** Identify customer problem and objective evidence from interviews.
2. **CTO / specialist advisers:** Document physics, standards questions, assumptions and major technical risks.
3. **CFO / COO:** Evaluate resource constraints and sequence only no-cost research actions.
4. **CLO / CISO:** Flag regulated, IP, contract and personal-data requirements for qualified review.
5. **CMO / design:** Ensure a premium, minimal visual presentation without implying validated hardware.
6. **CSO / CEO:** Choose continue, narrow, pause or stop; founder confirms the decision.

## Website product-experience principles
- Lead with the **image and product name**, then **one short line** of meaning.
- Maintain the founder-selected image URLs, order and visual subjects unless explicitly requested otherwise.
- Keep the website's disclaimer that all products are concept-stage; no performance, certification, funding or customer claim without evidence.
- Preserve English/Greek support, mobile layout and accessible contrast.
- Do not use invented pricing, specifications, build dates or partner logos.

## Success criteria
Evidence clarity, qualified interest, safety readiness, realistic capital needs and actionable next decisions matter more than page count or marketing adjectives.

**Boundaries:** This virtual council cannot confer legal titles, technical sign-offs, certification, contracts or statutory compliance. Paid work and safety-critical operations require qualified external professionals and explicit founder approval.
