# VELTARO — From Website to First Customers and Production (Launch Gates)
**22 October 2026 · Execution plan, not evidence of an incorporated company or certified vehicle**

## Current reality
VELTARO V1 is a concept, with no validated aircraft, road approval, certified design, production line, price, customer orders, or delivery timeline. `early-interest.html` is a **research inquiry**, not a deposit, pre-order, paid waitlist, or guaranteed response. Do not accept vehicle money or advertise delivery dates.

## Stage 1 — Real-world demand (first 30 days after compliant launch)
- Define one user segment and one constrained, legal origin-to-destination journey; avoid claims of street takeoff.
- Interview 12–20 consenting target users, aviation operators and qualified domain experts. Record problem frequency, current solution, cost/time constraints and willingness-to-pay hypotheses (not promised purchases).
- Maintain a research CRM with inquiry ID, acquisition channel, consent date, status, follow-up date, opt-out/deletion state and interview evidence. Limit access and retention.
- Funnel: website visit → research inquiry → consented qualification → interview → evidence summary. Track conversion without treating inquiries as orders.
- Gate: at least one repeating painful use case and evidence that real users would prefer a legally feasible solution to current alternatives. If not, revise mission rather than advertising a vehicle.

## Stage 2 — Engineering feasibility (months 2–6; estimate only)
- Agree vehicle jurisdiction and qualified aviation/automotive advisors.
- Compare runway/folding-wing and powered-lift VTOL using a **single** mission and source-backed pack-level specific energy, peak power, subsystem mass and realistic reserves.
- Review failure modes, legal departure and arrival sites, emergency operations, human factors, thermal margins and certifiability.
- Gate: independently reviewed mass–energy/power feasibility, lawful mission path and risk review. If evidence is insufficient, HOLD BOTH. Do not build or fly.

## Stage 3 — Company formation / investability (parallel with stages 1–2)
- Obtain accounting/legal advice on company registration, jurisdiction, founder agreements, trademark searches, IP assignments, liability, insurance, privacy and financial controls.
- Build a milestone-based budget for feasibility and an evidence-based investor memo. Separate research services revenue (if any) from hypothetical vehicle sales.
- Verify any fundraising, solicitation or customer contract requirements with counsel. No invented investor commitments.
- Gate: coherent legal entity plan, team capability, budget, risk register, measurable next milestone and transparent claims.

## Stage 4 — Prototype / validation / industrialization (later, contingent; not scheduled promises)
- Engage qualified design organizations and relevant aviation/road authorities early to define certification basis.
- Progress through design review, analysis, safe ground rigs, qualified component validation, integration, regulated testing, production quality system, supplier traceability, manufacturing readiness, aftersales/support.
- Only after applicable approvals and reliable production/cost evidence consider customer orders and delivery promises.
- Gate: no production launch without regulatory compliance, validated design, manufacturing capacity, quality control and adequate capital.

## Weekly dashboard
Visitors; research inquiry conversions; consented qualified leads; interviews completed; demonstrated user problems; evidence-backed technical gates closed; open critical regulatory/safety risks; available cash runway (if applicable). Never report research leads as paid customers.

## Ownership
Assign explicit human owners for legal/privacy, engineering, customer research, form inbox, deployment and finances before public launch. No task in this document means a company has been formed, customers acquired, an investment raised or a vehicle authorized.
