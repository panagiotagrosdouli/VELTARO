## Verification ladder
1. Source-grounded requirements and first-order analyses.
2. Independent review of physics and hazards by competent professionals.
3. Controlled simulations or engineering models only after credible inputs.
4. Physical verification, road tests or flight work **only after separate safety, legal and funding authorizations**.

Evidence types are intentionally separate: passing a static code test does not certify flight safety.

## Release governance
No public numerical performance claims, certification statements, customer promises or flight schedule based on these unknowns. No change to founder-selected Products/SkyCourier images without consent.
