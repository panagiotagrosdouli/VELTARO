## Honest customer-facing offer — draft
**VELTARO LABS — Preliminary Mobility Feasibility Brief**
A scoped, desk-based engineering research report for early mobility concepts. Possible sections: mission definition, assumption register, high-level requirements, qualitative safety/dependency map, first-order models with provenance, open uncertainties and recommended next studies. Every engagement must explicitly distinguish modelling, validated analysis, and regulated engineering services. No design signoff, certification, operational permission, or guarantee of performance is included.

## Customer discovery interview script (no fake traction)
1. What mobility or system-integration problem is expensive or slow today?
2. How do you currently evaluate the early feasibility of such concepts?
3. Who decides budget and who approves vendors?
4. What deliverable would be useful in the next month?
5. What evidence or credentials would make a provider credible?
6. Would you consider a scoped paid desk study? What procurement requirements apply?
Record interview date, segment, consent, verbatim objections, value signals, next step. Do not report interested contacts as paying clients.
