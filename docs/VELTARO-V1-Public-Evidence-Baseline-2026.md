# VELTARO V1 — Public-source regulatory baseline
**Checked 19 October 2026. Research only: applicability NOT determined for V1.**

## Source 1 — EASA CS-23 catalog (track A research)
- URL: https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/certification-specifications/group/cs-23-normal-utility-aerobatic-and-commuter-aeroplanes
- Publisher: European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA).
- Public fact: EASA publishes specifications for normal-category aeroplanes, including a 2026 update listed in its catalog.
- Relevance: examine **whether any conventional fixed-wing aviation route is applicable** to a roadable winged concept.
- Boundary: **does not** establish that a roadable/folding-wing design qualifies under CS-23, or that road-use approval is possible.
- Classification: SOURCED REFERENCE; not reviewed legal applicability.

## Source 2 — EASA SC-VTOL and MoC catalog (track B research)
- URL: https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/product-certification-consultations/special-condition-vtol
- Publisher: EASA.
- Public fact: the page lists SC-VTOL Issue 2 and means-of-compliance publications/consultations, including the 2025 proposed MOC-5 consultation.
- Relevance: possible source for VTOL certification topics requiring specialist regulatory interpretation.
- Boundary: proposal vs final publication must be differentiated; no implication that V1 would meet any condition.
- Classification: SOURCED REFERENCE; not reviewed legal applicability.

## Source 3 — FAA powered-lift pilot and operations rules (track B / jurisdiction comparator)
- URL: https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/integration-powered-lift-pilot-certification-and-operations-miscellaneous-amendments
- Publisher: United States Federal Aviation Administration (FAA).
- Public fact: October 2024 final rule sets frameworks for powered-lift pilot qualification and operations within the **US** system.
- Relevance: track distinctions among aircraft classification, pilot qualifications, operational approvals and site access.
- Boundary: US rule is not EU/Greece law; it does not certify a VELTARO aircraft.
- Classification: SOURCED REFERENCE; not validated for V1.
