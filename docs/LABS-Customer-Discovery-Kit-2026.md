# VELTARO LABS — Customer discovery kit v1.0
**Prepared 19 October 2026 | Concept-stage | No customers, partnerships or paid services claimed**

## Purpose
Test whether engineering-oriented teams need a traceable, non-regulated desk research brief. This is **research outreach**, not an offer of certified engineering services.

## Target groups (hypotheses, not a contact list)
- Early-stage mobility and robotics development teams
- Applied research laboratories and academic technology-transfer groups
- SMEs exploring battery, materials, automation or logistics innovations

Do not list private personal emails or names in this public repository. Use publicly available organization contact routes and respect requests not to contact again.

## Qualification checklist
1. Has the organization recently evaluated a technical market or system question?
2. Is a cited evidence-and-assumption brief useful to its workflow?
3. Does it have a real decision owner and a defined timeline?
4. Is the work non-confidential, desk-based and within demonstrated competence?
5. Could a future commercial arrangement be lawful in the relevant jurisdiction?

## Interview guide — 15–20 minutes
1. What was the most recent decision where missing research slowed your team?
2. What sources or consultants did you use instead?
3. What format made the result actionable?
4. What was unsatisfactory about the existing approach?
5. Who would decide whether to commission a similar brief?
6. May we follow up once with a public, non-confidential example?

Do not pitch prices first. Record what interviewees actually say, not inferred purchase intent.

## Outreach message template (requires human review)
**Subject:** Short research question — early-stage mobility analysis

Hello,

I'm exploring whether concise, source-backed technical landscape briefs would help teams making early mobility and engineering decisions. VELTARO LABS is still at the research stage; this is not a sales offer.

Would your team be willing to share how you currently gather and evaluate public technical evidence? A brief conversation would help us avoid building something nobody needs.

No confidential material is requested. If this is not relevant, no reply is necessary.

Kind regards,
VELTARO research initiative

## Evidence gates
- Goal: 3 substantive LABS discovery discussions; not yet achieved.
- At least 2 independent reports of a similar concrete problem are a reason to refine the offer, **not** proof of willingness to pay.
- If no concrete problem emerges, narrow the hypothesis or stop.
- No invoice, deposit or paid client engagement before lawful structure, tax and professional scope are checked.

## Publication checklist
Ensure the sample report has source attribution, assumptions, limitations and no implied certification. Do not name prospects as partners or customers without explicit authorization.
