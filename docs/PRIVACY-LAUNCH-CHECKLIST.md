# Privacy launch checklist

The published `privacy.html` is a provisional notice. Do not describe the website as GDPR-compliant until the controller has supplied the missing facts and the final notice has received appropriate legal review.

## Owner decisions

- [ ] Confirm the legal person or entity acting as data controller and the public contact address.
- [ ] Confirm and document a lawful basis for general inquiries and customer research; distinguish any separate optional purpose.
- [ ] Set a retention period for the destination mailbox, FormSubmit submissions, and any exported research notes; define deletion practice.
- [ ] Review FormSubmit's current privacy terms, processing documentation, subprocessors, security information, and international transfer mechanisms. Decide whether its service is suitable for the intended data.
- [ ] Confirm who can access the receiving mailbox and apply appropriate access controls.
- [ ] Define how access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, and complaint requests will be handled, where applicable.
- [ ] Confirm whether any research responses will be retained or analyzed beyond replying to the inquiry; update forms and notice if so.
- [ ] Verify the deployed site and third-party resources for cookies, analytics, embedded content, or other tracking. Add disclosures or consent only if actual technologies and applicable rules require them.
- [ ] Confirm permission for the four externally hosted Unsplash images or replace them with owned/licensed assets. Account for the image host's request data in the final notice.
- [ ] Review the finalized notice with qualified privacy counsel before wider public outreach.

## Current known processing from source review

- General contact form: name, email address, free-text message, and a required reply/contact consent checkbox.
- Research form: name, email, broad user profile, use-case category, free-text response, and a required contact-consent checkbox.
- The HTML posts form fields to FormSubmit for email delivery to the configured Gmail destination. Inbox activation and real delivery have not been tested.
- Homepage photographs are requested from Unsplash image servers. No analytics or advertising scripts were identified in the reviewed website files; verify the deployed site before making a final statement.
- No legal controller identity, lawful basis, retention schedule, formal processor arrangement, or transfer safeguards were verified.
