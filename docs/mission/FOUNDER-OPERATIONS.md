## Discovery and outreach workflow
No outreach sent. With founder authorization: select an account and research purpose → verify appropriate contact and legal basis → send a factual bounded question → record consent and objections → follow up only when appropriate → erase on request. Never convert an expression of interest into a customer claim.

Interview template: date / segment / consent / actual recent problem / current workaround / frequency and cost with provenance / budget owner / procurement requirements / verbatim objection / willingness-to-pay evidence / next step / retention review.

Pipeline stages: researched → authorized outreach → conversation consented → problem evidenced → scope agreed → lawful contract → delivery review → accepted. All current counts UNKNOWN, not invented zero-customer traction statistics.

## Engineering review responsibilities
Founder accepts scope and resource decisions. Software reviewer checks reproducibility/security. Qualified robotics reviewer (not the V1 signoff role) or aerospace/automotive reviewer (required) reviews physical assumptions and hazards. Privacy/legal reviewer approves notices and terms. Accountant reviews taxes/costs. Roles are responsibilities, not staffed employees.

## SOPs
1. Every numerical claim gets units, assumption/measurement label, source/date and review status.
2. Every release runs full tests, records failures and checks deployed revision separately.
3. Every expenditure, contract, legal filing or external communication needs explicit authorization.
4. Incidents: inhibit affected workflow, preserve minimal logs, notify founder, investigate and independently review before resuming.
5. Client deliverables: bounded question, public evidence register, assumptions, calculations, uncertainty, independent review, limitations and next validation.
