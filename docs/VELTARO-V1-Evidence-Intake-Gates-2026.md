# VELTARO V1 — Engineering evidence intake checklist
**19 October 2026 | No source-derived subsystem estimates entered yet**

| Evidence item | Acceptance criteria | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Defined customer trip | Actual road legs + permissible aviation sites; operating jurisdiction stated | OPEN |
| Primary occupant/payload requirement | Documented user hypothesis and load convention | OPEN |
| Mass accounting boundaries | No double counting of battery casing, cooling, structural interfaces | OPEN |
| VTOL rotor installation assumptions | Disc area, efficiency, hover/transition duration, failure conditions | OPEN |
| Folding fixed-wing assumptions | Wing performance, takeoff/landing constraints, fold-lock structure mass | OPEN |
| Energy system evidence | Pack-level usable Wh/kg and peak/continuous output with source conditions | OPEN |
| Ground operating model | Road propulsion, road legal mass/envelope and structural requirements | OPEN |
| Operational constraints | Airspace, facilities, required training, weather, reserves | OPEN |
| Safety review pathway | Independent qualified specialist identified for key hazards | OPEN |
| Data provenance | Source, date, scope, uncertainty, reviewer for each future number | OPEN |

## Stop/go
If technical sources do not establish a plausible complete mass and energy ledger or a safe and legal operation, **pause the concept rather than announce a viable flying car**.

This checklist tracks missing evidence, not experimental findings.
