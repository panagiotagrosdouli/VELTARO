## Research area: Alexandroupolis / Thrace, Greece

Why: the documented **Alexandroupolis Airport “Dimokritos” (LGAL)** provides a publicly traceable starting point to investigate possible runway-based operations. Its existence and aerodrome documentation **do not establish that a proposed roadable aircraft may operate there**.

**Official sources**
- Hellenic Civil Aviation Authority/HASP airport listing: https://www.hcaa.gr/en/our-airports/kratikos-aerolimenas-alejandroypolhs-dhmokritos-kaald
- Greek AIP aeronautical information for LGAL (publication referenced; check latest effective amendment before any operational analysis): https://aisgr.hasp.gov.gr/aipgr_incl_amdt_0326_wef_16apr2026/cd/ais/eaip/pdf/AD%202/AD2-LGAL/LG_AD_2_LGAL_en.pdf
- HCAA aerodrome operators: https://hcaa.gov.gr/en/foreis-leitoyrgias-aerodromion
