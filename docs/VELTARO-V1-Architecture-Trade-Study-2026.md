# VELTARO V1 — Architecture trade study (Rev 0)
**19 October 2026 | Internal concept study | No validated aircraft or road product**

## Decision being investigated
Compare **A. winged roadable aircraft with ground-to-air configuration changes** against **B. powered-lift / VTOL roadable vehicle**. These are *architecture classes*, not endorsed designs. Neither is currently evidenced as feasible, certifiable, or affordable.

## Decision criteria and evidence questions

| Criterion | A — winged / runway-dependent | B — powered lift / VTOL | Evidence required | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Legal operational access | Needs lawful takeoff/landing areas and road route | Needs approved vertiports or equivalent and legal road route | Exact jurisdiction and facility availability | UNKNOWN |
| Mission access | Ground leg plus airfield access may dominate | Shorter facility access is possible, not assured | End-to-end trip model | UNKNOWN |

**No scores or weighted rankings:** The unresolved operating mission, regulatory regime, validated models and sources make numerical ranking misleading.

## Required gating sequence
1. Define *one* plausible legal journey (origin, road stage, departure site, air stage, arrival site, destination) and the target jurisdiction.
2. Resolve where and by whom the vehicle could be flown and driven, and what authorization pathways apply; obtain qualified professional review.
3. Define occupant and luggage needs, adverse weather envelope, operational days, and failure cases.
4. Build separate **mass and power** evidence ledgers for A and B, with pack-level energy and power uncertainty, not cell-level marketing figures.
5. Analyze safety hazards including loss of power, rejected takeoff/landing, conversion failures, protection of occupants and bystanders, and road crash scenarios.
6. Compare door-to-door utility using validated assumptions with explicit uncertainty; stop if legal access is not evidenced.
7. Only then create a decision matrix with sourced scores, sensitivity cases and independent review.

## Decision at this gate
**HOLD — BOTH ARCHITECTURES.** Neither architecture is selected, no performance figures or flight-readiness representations are approved. Next artifact is an evidence-backed mission definition, *not* a rendering, supplier solicitation or real-world test.
