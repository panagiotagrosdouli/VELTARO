# VELTARO LABS — Research sample: comparing mobility concepts
**19 October 2026 | Demonstration report only | Not commissioned, certified or peer reviewed**

## Research question
What should an early-stage mobility team examine before preferring an electric VTOL configuration over a runway-based fixed-wing configuration for a road-capable concept?

## Method and scope
This is an illustrative desktop research brief demonstrating how LABS could structure a transparent early feasibility investigation. No proprietary data, actual hardware tests, simulations, customer studies or aerodynamic calculations were performed.

| Dimension | VTOL: questions to resolve | Runway / folding fixed-wing: questions to resolve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mission | How long does hover/transition take? What landing sites are authorized? | What runway/landing access exists and how does it affect journeys? |
| Mass | Rotor/motor installation, protection and redundancy mass? | Wings, folding joints, locks and structural reinforcement mass? |
| Energy | Hover and transition power, thermal constraints, flight reserves? | Takeoff/climb, cruise energy, reserve and ground-drive needs? |
| Safety | Propulsion failures, controllability and bystander risk? | Folding mechanism failure, stall/landing risk and road conversion interlocks? |
| Regulation | Which aircraft category, operational approval and road approval might apply? | Which airworthiness/road approval regime and aerodrome restrictions might apply? |
| Viability | Who needs this mission, and what alternatives already solve it? | Does restricted runway access defeat the customer proposition? |

## Reproducible work product
A future study should specify mission distance, occupants, payload, component assumptions, design point, reserves, and governing constraints, then estimate and independently review mass, energy, installed power and safety risks. A side-by-side comparison is not meaningful unless both architectures use the same mission assumptions.

## Important limitations
No conclusion on comparative feasibility, actual mass, power, speed, price, range, certification or safe operation is supported by this sample. Independent qualified aerospace, automotive and regulatory experts would be needed before engineering validation or physical tests.
