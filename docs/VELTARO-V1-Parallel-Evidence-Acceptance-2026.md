# VELTARO V1 — Parallel architecture evidence acceptance gates (Rev 1)
**19 October 2026 · Internal research · Unverified concepts, not operational designs**

The winged/runway-dependent (A) and powered-lift VTOL (B) tracks both continue. For each task below, acceptance means **traceable evidence and an expert review plan**, not a declaration of feasibility.

| Gate | A — folding wing / runway | B — powered lift / VTOL | Evidence acceptance | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 Operational access | Lawful runway and road connection both ends | Lawful vertical takeoff/landing facility and road link both ends | Specific jurisdiction, facility requirements, legal review, door-to-door itinerary | BOTH OPEN |
| G2 Mass closure | Road structure, wing, fold-lock, powertrain, margin | Road structure, rotors, guard/interlocks, controllers, margin | Mutually exclusive subsystem ledger, sourced boundaries and uncertainty | BOTH OPEN |
| G3 Energy + power | Takeoff/climb/cruise/landing/contingency | Hover/transition/cruise/landing/contingency | Phase-specific loads, pack usable energy and discharge power, thermal assumptions | BOTH OPEN |

| G5 Usability | Drive-runway-aviation-runway-drive | Drive-facility-aviation-facility-drive | Realistic user trip and lawful infrastructure, no invented time savings | BOTH OPEN |
| G6 Compliance | Road approval + relevant aviation category | Road approval + relevant powered-lift classification | Jurisdictional legal opinion and qualified aviation review | BOTH OPEN |

## Evidence schema for future inputs
For each claim: track, gate, unique reference, source title/URL or document ID, publication date, retrieval date, quantitative scope, units, system boundary, uncertainty interval, reviewer and a **claim-status** of UNSOURCED / SOURCED / REVIEWED / REJECTED. Status does not imply certification.

## Automatic stop conditions
- No lawful departure and arrival route → **cannot advance operational value assumptions**.
- Pack peak/sustained power or usable energy missing → **do not publish mission range**.
- No complete road + aircraft mass ledger → **do not publish takeoff mass**.
- No qualified safety review → **do not represent passenger or bystander safety as verified**.
- Unsupported source or incompatible measurement boundary → **reject input and document why**.

**Both remain OPEN. No preferred architecture, no hardware validation, no purchase or external contact authorization.**
