# VELTARO V1 — Engineering feasibility brief

**Status:** Proposed research agenda. Not a design certification, prototype specification, flight authorization, or assertion of validated performance.

## System boundaries
A dual road/air configuration must satisfy two distinct design environments and operating regulatory frameworks. Before committing to an architecture, document the intended mission, payload, road dimensions, air operating envelope, takeoff/landing concept, pilot requirements, charging needs and reserve policies.

## Essential studies
1. **Mass budget:** empty mass, payload, powertrain, energy storage, structural margins, ground equipment and contingency.
2. **Energy/power budget:** takeoff peak power, climb, cruise, reserve, system losses, battery derating and worst-case operating temperatures.
3. **Aerodynamics:** lift/drag analyses, crosswind response, effects of folding structures and ground proximity.
4. **Flight controls:** stability, sensor failure, actuator failures, degraded modes, manual/automatic control and safety monitoring.
5. **Structures:** crashworthiness, rotor containment, vibration, fatigue, landing and automotive impact loads.
6. **Noise and human factors:** community noise, emergency ingress/egress, pilot training, workload, situational awareness and maintenance.

## Verification roadmap
Requirements → simulation → subsystem rig tests → integrated ground tests → independent safety review → approved flight tests → certification evidence. Each transition requires documented go/no-go criteria.

## Regulatory framing
Consult qualified specialists and relevant competent authorities; see [EASA](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en). Vehicle category, intended operation and jurisdiction determine the applicable compliance route. Road homologation is a separate question. No approval has been obtained or implied.

## Open decisions
- Does a single dual-mode vehicle offer a better mission outcome than separate car and aircraft?
- How much road-use mass and volume reduce useful flight performance?
- What takeoff/landing infrastructure would be required?
- What weather, licensing and operational limits apply?
- Can cost, reliability and environmental outcomes be supported by independent evidence?

All quantitative performance claims must remain **TBD** until supported by calculations or test evidence.
