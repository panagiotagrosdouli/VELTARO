# VELTARO — First 10 Research Conversations
**Pre-incorporation concept-stage plan | 22 October 2026**

## Goal and limits
Validate genuine mobility problems through **10 voluntary discovery conversations**, not bookings, preorders, deposits or fabricated leads. The V1 concept has no demonstrated prototype, published certification or production offer.

## Seven-day execution
- Day 1: Verify FormSubmit activation and actual delivery to the founder-controlled receiving mailbox using two non-sensitive test messages. Do not launch outreach if emails do not arrive.
- Day 2: Review privacy notice, determine controller identity, lawful basis, retention and FormSubmit processing arrangements with qualified assistance. Audit image licenses and replace any unlicensed externally hosted imagery **without discarding the original design**.
- Day 3: Create an opt-in outreach list from people who explicitly agreed to be contacted, stored in a secured private tool, **not GitHub**. Do not scrape or purchase personal email lists.
- Days 4–5: Personally invite up to 10 appropriately matched individuals in professional networks to a 15-minute discovery call; do not promise availability or send mass unsolicited email.
- Days 6–7: Summarize anonymized recurring problems, alternatives and willingness to explore research; decide what evidence to gather next.

## Suggested interview questions
1. What journey or delivery job is hardest to complete today?
2. What transportation method do you use, and what is the actual cost/time/risk?
3. How frequently does this problem occur?
4. What would have to be true for a road-and-air or other mobility solution to be useful?
5. What safety, noise, licensing, infrastructure and reliability constraints are non-negotiable?
6. Would you volunteer for a second research interview? Record permission independently.

## Internal lead stages (private records only)
**New inquiry → Consent/notice checked → Needs clarification → Discovery invited → Interview completed → Research closed.**
Never create a public GitHub issue with personal information. Limit access, set and honor a retention rule once legally reviewed; do not equate unanswered queries with customer demand.
