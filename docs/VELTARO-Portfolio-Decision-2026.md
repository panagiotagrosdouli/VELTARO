## Initial regulatory research boundaries
- Road-vehicle approval in the EU is governed by a framework including Regulation (EU) 2018/858; applicability to a proposed dual-use architecture must be determined by qualified advisors. Official: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32018R0858
- EU civil drone operations fall within risk-based 'open', 'specific', and 'certified' categories. Delivery missions cannot assume 'open' category permissions, especially where dropping materials, BVLOS or other constraints apply. Official EASA: https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/drones-air-mobility/operating-drone
- EASA UAS detailed rules: https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/easy-access-rules/online-publications/easy-access-rules-unmanned-aircraft-systems
- An aircraft concept and road-vehicle concept may trigger distinct technical, regulatory and operational responsibilities. This memo does not determine certification basis or permit flight tests.

## Commercial experiment
1. Select one narrow LABS example: literature-and-competitor landscape for a publicly documented mobility subsystem; use only permitted published sources.
2. Define scope, exclusions and method; label all unverified assumptions.
3. Ask 3 suitable prospects about an actual recent problem, existing workaround and purchase decision process. Don't pitch a made-up product.
4. If all feedback is vague, revise or stop. No invoice or client deliverable until lawful structure, tax and competency are verified.
