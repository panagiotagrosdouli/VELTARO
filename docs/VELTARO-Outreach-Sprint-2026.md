# VELTARO — First research outreach sprint (internal instructions)
**Week 1–2 | €0 planned | No external outreach sent as part of this document**

## Goal
Identify organizations rather than invent contacts, then secure at most two initial voluntary discovery conversations. This is market research, not partnership solicitation or a sales campaign.

## Public-source candidate categories
| Category | What to look for | Why it matters | Qualification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| University applied engineering research groups | Published work in systems engineering, robotics, vehicle architecture | Discuss publicly known feasibility methodologies | Group has an explicit public inquiry channel |
| Technology transfer or incubator programs | Public pages describing mobility / hardware startup support | Understand evidence expected from pre-incorporation ventures | Clear startup intake instructions |
| Small engineering consultancies | Public technical market-study or R&D offerings | Compare existing alternatives for a LABS research brief | No confidential client data involved |
| Robotics or logistics operators | Public case studies in constrained deliveries | Validate distinct SKYCOURIER/GROUND problem hypotheses | Problem-owner role identifiable |

## Practical sequence
1. Gather 12 organization **names and publicly provided organization inquiry URLs**; review relevance first. Do not publish personal emails.
2. Select 3 LABS candidates for a **manual, opt-in approach** using the existing discovery kit.
3. When contact is welcome, send a short factual message with no unverified claims. Record whether they reply; silence is not interest.
4. Ask about their most recent actual research problem and how they handled it. Collect only necessary notes with consent and retention controls.
5. After evidence exists, revisit the executive portfolio memo; avoid assigning 0–5 scores to unsupported hypotheses.

## What must not be claimed
No incorporated entity, funded team, certified engineering service, prototype, investor commitments or paid client base. Do not send bulk unsolicited email; do not share sensitive data via public GitHub issues.
