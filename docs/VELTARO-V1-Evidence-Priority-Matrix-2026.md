# VELTARO V1 — Evidence acquisition priority matrix

**20 October 2026 · Concept research · No product performance validated**

The prior 24 educational sensitivity cases explain *which assumptions matter mathematically* but **cannot establish real-world ranking or flight feasibility**. This matrix turns open assumptions into an evidence backlog, not a procurement request.

## Priority basis
- **P0**: prevents any meaningful architecture comparison or lawful mission definition.
- **P1**: crucial for architecture-specific safety/performance assessment once P0 scope exists.
- **P2**: strengthens mission and commercial evaluation after scope is credible.

Priority reflects **research sequencing**, not a scored safety risk or confirmed technical feasibility.

| Priority | Evidence to obtain | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | Legal site/access and aircraft/road regulatory scope | Without legal mission, theoretical energy has no operating context |
| P0 | Usable installed pack-level Wh/kg | Cell marketing energy density is not usable aircraft pack data |
| P0 | Relevant peak and sustained power | VTOL hover/transition and pack thermal constraints cannot be inferred from energy alone |
| P0 | Reviewed dry/subsystem mass ledger | Aircraft and battery mass are strongly coupled |
| P1 | Runway and wing-borne phase performance | Needed to validate transforming-wing candidate |
| P1 | Propulsor efficiencies | Missing phase-specific electrical demand |
| P1 | Locking structure evidence | Conversion integrity needs separate qualified assurance |
| P1 | Independent controls/power common-cause review | Shared failures cannot be resolved by duplicate diagrams |
| P2 | Site-specific duration, weather and reserve envelope | Actual M-01 cases remain undefined |
| P2 | Cost and maintenance evidence | No defensible business-case comparison yet |
