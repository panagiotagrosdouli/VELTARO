## Weekly operating rhythm
- 2 h desk engineering/research, 2 h problem discovery, 1 h evidence and website integrity, 1 h relationship-building. These are optional targets.
- Monday: choose 1–2 measurable outputs. Friday: mark complete only with links or files.
- Monthly: update the decision log and verify privacy, consent, and public-claims accuracy.

## KPI definitions
- **Qualified interview:** direct conversation with a relevant user/operator/expert with dated notes and permission to retain notes.
- **Validated assumption:** substantiated by an identifiable source or repeatable calculation; label uncertainty.
- **Customer interest:** not a sale, contract, or committed funding.
- **Completed deliverable:** accessible artifact in repo or private evidence log; avoid publishing contact personal data.
