# VELTARO — Zero-Capital Operating Plan v0.2
**Date:** 2026-10-18
**Founder's confirmed available capital: €0.**
**Jurisdiction: Undecided.**
This document supersedes any suggestion to spend €610, €1,000 or more in the previous planning scenario. No expenditure is authorized.

## Strategy
One pre-incorporation research initiative, four discovery tracks. No prototype manufacture, aircraft flight tests, paid staffing, paid subscriptions, incorporation, domain purchase, regulatory filings, customer deposits or unsupported flight claims.

| Track | Free deliverable | Evidence gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VELTARO V1 | First-principles comparison of eVTOL and folding fixed-wing vehicle concepts | Mass/energy assumptions with sources, preliminary hazard register |
| VELTARO AIR | Passenger/mission definition, regulatory questions, public literature review | 5 credible mission/customer insights |
| SKYCOURIER | Delivery use cases, stakeholder interviews, unit economics worksheet without assumed permits | 3 operator interviews and feasible mission statement |
| VELTARO LABS | Public non-confidential study sample, portfolio of skills and small service offer hypothesis | Positive feedback from 3 potential clients; legal ability to invoice confirmed before paid work |

## 12-month €0 base-case cash plan
- Initial cash: **€0**
- Committed cash spending: **€0** in each of the 12 months.
- Guaranteed revenue: **€0** in each of the 12 months.
- Cash at end of each month: **€0**.
- Founder time and use of already-available devices/connectivity are in-kind contributions, not cash costs.
- Any revenue opportunity requires a separate plan for contracting, tax/accounting and legal compliance. Do not recognize hypothetical revenue as forecast.
- Any expense requires funding and explicit fresh approval; otherwise delay the expense.

## First 90 days
**Days 1–30:** Verify website claims and licensing, create one-page brief for each track, inventory own skills, collect 12 outreach leads, conduct 4 exploratory interviews.
**Days 31–60:** 4 more interviews, source-grounded technical and market briefs, initial legal/regulatory research with clear limitations, public LABS sample report.
**Days 61–90:** 4 more interviews, score 4 workstreams for customer evidence, feasibility, team fit and need for capital; decide which paid deliverable or non-dilutive opportunity to validate first.

## Months 4–6
Build relationships with academic labs, incubators and potential service buyers. Prepare a short concept-stage deck using verified facts only. Do not assume program eligibility or awards.

## Months 7–9
Investigate grant/incubator calls that are actually open at that time. Find external technical reviewers who may offer an introductory discussion; don't expect free professional services.

## Months 10–12
Complete decision memo: continue unfunded research, obtain financing, or limit focus to revenue-generating LABS work. Decide incorporation country only when real operations or a concrete funding/contract opportunity warrants professional advice.

## Decision gates
1. **No spend without funds.**
2. **No invoicing or contracting without confirming a lawful structure in the relevant jurisdiction.**
3. **No regulated engineering assurance or aviation operations without competent licensed specialists and approvals.**
4. **No public customer, partner, prototype, patent or financing claims without evidence.**
5. **No fabricated market size, launch date, flight range or performance.**

## Weekly cadence
- 2 hours technical research with citations.
- 2 hours customer/problem discovery.
- 1 hour documentation and website integrity.
- 1 hour relationship-building.
These are optional time targets, not proof of founder availability.
