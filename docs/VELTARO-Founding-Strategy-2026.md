# VELTARO — Founding strategy v0.1
**Date:** 2026-10-18 | **Status:** Proposal / pre-incorporation | **Jurisdiction:** Undecided

## Corporate thesis
Explore advanced personal and industrial mobility at the intersection of automotive engineering, aviation and autonomy. Start as **one proposed company/brand with four research workstreams**, not four legal subsidiaries. Do not claim existing hardware, IP rights, funding, or regulatory approvals.

## Portfolio
| Workstream | Initial problem/hypothesis | 90-day output | Critical gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VELTARO V1 | Can a premium vehicle practically combine road legality and controlled flight? | Side-by-side eVTOL / folding fixed-wing mass, energy, safety, road/air regulatory study | Physical and regulatory feasibility |
| VELTARO AIR | Is there a narrow customer mission for compact aircraft? | Customer/mission study, system-level feasibility outline | Clear paying user and plausible certification path |
| SKYCOURIER | Is there a commercially viable, safe use case for last-mile delivery drones? | Use-case interviews, airspace/operations/legal and unit economics study | Positive unit-economics hypothesis under real restrictions |
| VELTARO LABS | Can engineering research and services create near-term value? | Defined research capabilities, first demonstrable analysis/report, 3 potential customers/partners interviewed | Credible deliverable and willingness to pay |

## Portfolio allocation during discovery (time, not funding)
V1 30%; AIR 20%; SKYCOURIER 25%; LABS 25%. Rebalance after evidence review; these are provisional management assumptions. No program moves to prototyping by default.

## Positioning
"VELTARO is an early-stage mobility research venture investigating safer, technically credible road-and-air transport solutions." Avoid claiming market leadership, functioning aircraft, validated specs or launch deadlines.

## Governance
Founder: portfolio decisions, evidence register, approvals. Aerospace and automotive specialists: external design review before any prototype spend. Legal/accounting advisors: incorporation and IP. Keep a decision log and source-backed claims register; collect explicit consent for contact data.

## 90-day milestones
**Days 1–30:** 12 structured discovery interviews across the portfolio, 1-page briefs per workstream, baseline technical assumptions, 3-country jurisdiction screen, website/privacy audit.
**Days 31–60:** V1 dual-architecture first-order feasibility, AIR mission brief, SKYCOURIER regulatory+cost model, LABS service test; independent technical review of highest-risk assumptions.
**Days 61–90:** compare four workstreams on customer pull, feasible regulation, technical risk, capital intensity, and team access; select ONE funded pilot/study and park the others; approve 12-month plan.

## Evidence-based gate (each 0–5)
Score customer demand (25%), regulatory feasibility (20%), technical feasibility (25%), capital efficiency (20%), and access to talent/partners (10%). Scores must cite evidence and uncertainty; missing evidence is not assigned a positive score. Do not imply these weights are an external standard.

## Public integrity
Only publish verified facts. Concepts are illustrative. Do not take deposits, make forward-looking performance promises, or conduct flight tests without qualified legal, safety and regulatory review.
