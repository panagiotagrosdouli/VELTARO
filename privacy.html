How website inquiries are handled

Important: This is a provisional contact-data explanation, not a finalized legal privacy policy. Confirm the operating legal entity/controller, contact details, retention, lawful basis and processor arrangements with a qualified advisor before public launch.

Information visitors may submit

The website contact form requests a name, email address, area of interest and a free-text message. The separate early customer research form also asks for a broad user profile and mobility use case. Please do not send confidential, sensitive or unnecessary personal information.

Purpose and delivery

Form submissions are intended to let the project team receive and respond to inquiries. The website currently sends form data to the third-party FormSubmit service for delivery by email. Form submission may transfer the information to that service and associated infrastructure. Review FormSubmit's applicable privacy terms and processing documentation before using it.

Other third-party requests

The home page currently loads reference photographs from Unsplash image servers. Those requests may disclose technical information such as an IP address and browser request data to the image host. The photographs are references, not VELTARO products; confirm display and commercial usage rights before public promotion. The site has no analytics or advertising script in the reviewed HTML. Confirm this remains true before launch.

Storage, access and retention

Messages may remain in the destination mailbox and in systems involved in form delivery. FormSubmit's public FAQ states that it retains submissions for 30 days; the owner should confirm that this applies to the active service and review any separate mailbox retention. VELTARO has not yet set its own deletion schedule or formally confirmed mailbox access controls. Do not assume that a message is instantly deleted or encrypted end-to-end.

This provisional notice is not a finalized GDPR policy.
