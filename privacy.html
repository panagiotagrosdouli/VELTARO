How website inquiries are handled

Important: This is a provisional contact-data explanation, not a finalized legal privacy policy. Confirm the operating legal entity/controller, contact details, retention, lawful basis and processor arrangements with a qualified advisor before public launch.

Information visitors may submit

The website inquiry form requests a name, email address, area of interest and a free-text message. Please do not send confidential, sensitive or unnecessary personal information.

Purpose and delivery

Form submissions are intended to let the project team receive and respond to inquiries. The website currently sends form data to the third-party FormSubmit service for delivery by email. Form submission may transfer the information to that service and associated infrastructure. Review FormSubmit's published privacy information before using it.

Storage, access and retention

Messages may remain in the destination mailbox and in systems involved in form delivery. A fixed deletion schedule and access controls have not yet been formally established. Do not assume that a message is instantly deleted or encrypted end-to-end.

Your questions and requests

Before collecting public inquiries, VELTARO must publish verified contact details for data-related requests and identify the responsible controller. Until then, the form and this notice should not be treated as a fully compliant public intake process.

No sale or customer account

Sending an inquiry does not constitute an order, investment, partnership, reservation or commercial contract.
