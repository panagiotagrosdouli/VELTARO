VELTARO AUTOMOBILI / THE FIRST CONCEPT

V1

A car for the road. An ambition for the sky.

Discover the vision

V1 is an early design concept. Images are automotive/aviation references, not depictions of a manufactured VELTARO vehicle.

01 / THE IDEA

Not a car with wings attached.

We want to create a real car that can drive on public roads and, in the right conditions, fly. Both modes need to work on their own terms. That makes V1 a difficult engineering challenge — and precisely the problem we want to study.

01

Drive

A low, sculpted grand tourer designed around the driver, road safety and real-world usability.

02

Fly

A flight mode to be defined only after evaluating lift, power, weight and safe control.

03

Belong

A cabin that feels like a premium vehicle, not an experiment. Every control must earn its place.

02 / ROAD MODE

First, a car you actually want to drive.

Road mode should offer precise handling, comfortable everyday use and an interior with the calm confidence of a grand tourer. Dimensions, structure and crash protection must ultimately meet road requirements.

03 / FLIGHT MODE

And then, the sky.

We will study vertical-takeoff and other potential architectures before choosing a configuration. Battery mass, power demands, redundancy, noise and emergency options may change the design radically. We will not publish imaginary performance numbers.

04 / REALITY CHECK

The questions we need to answer.

Energy

Can a road-legal mass budget carry enough energy for useful flight with safe reserves?

Structure

Can we reconcile road crash protection, flight loads, packaging and weight?

Safety

Which failure-tolerant systems, landing options and operating limits are necessary?

Approval

How could one vehicle satisfy both the relevant aviation and road approval frameworks?

Status: concept only. No prototype, approved specifications, road homologation or aviation certification.
