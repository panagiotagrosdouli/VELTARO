# VELTARO proposed visual identity
12 November 2026 — original vector geometry; founder approval and trademark clearance pending.

Primary palette: background #10151d, accent #dfc398, text #f3f5f5, muted #b8c4c9. Minimal, precise mobility research; a folded V with converging paths.

Typeface: Arial / system sans serif, licensed by the user's operating system; Greek fallback sans-serif. Use 500 weight display titles, 400 body, 700 labels. Body 17px minimum; line height 1.7. Keep one icon-width clear space; wordmark minimum 160px web, icon 24px. Never distort or imply certification. Dark mark on dark canvas, light mark on light canvas; mono on dark. Palette contrast requires checking each actual foreground/background pairing.

SVG sources contain editable vector paths and system text, not outlined typography. Browser-exported PNGs are derived review assets. Templates are editable HTML/PPTX sources; business cards have no invented person or title. No registered mark, legal protection or commercial clearance is claimed.

Use “concept-stage research venture.” Label all renderings and simulations. Preserve existing product imagery; its rights remain a separate review gate. NOVERA and VELTARO are independent projects; no corporate relationship inferred.
