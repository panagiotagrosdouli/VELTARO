The opportunity

A question worth investigating

Customer problem

Assess whether a road-and-air platform creates meaningful door-to-door advantages for defined travel missions.

Technical thesis

Investigate the intersection of compact road-worthy vehicle design and aviation-grade propulsion, control and safety systems.

Business hypothesis

Explore niche premium mobility and licensing possibilities after independent technical and customer validation.

Key diligence questions

What must be proven?

A credible investment case requires verified engineering and financial evidence before claims of viability can be made.

Evidence package to develop

1. Mission profile and target customer research.

2. Vehicle feasibility and design trade studies.

3. Regulatory and certification pathway analysis.

4. Risk-based engineering milestones and independent validation.

5. Funding requirements, runway, unit economics and development schedule.

Partnership pathways

Who might contribute?

Aerospace researchers

Flight dynamics, propulsion, aerodynamic and safety case expertise.

Automotive engineers

Road safety, packaging, ergonomics, manufacturing and homologation expertise.

Mobility stakeholders

Operations, infrastructure, customer discovery, financing and regulatory insight.
