Transparent development plan

Progress by proof,
not by promises.

An eight-stage proposed pathway from vision to possible commercial readiness. All stages are planning milestones; progress and dates are not claimed.

Proposed stage gates

The journey

  1. 01

    Concept definition

    Define vehicle mission, customer scenario, safety targets, preliminary requirements and constraints.

  2. 02

    Feasibility

    Compare architectures with mass, energy, aerodynamic, regulatory and economic trade studies. Gate: independent feasibility review.

  3. 03

    Simulation

    Multidisciplinary models of propulsion, controls, structures and failure cases. Gate: traceable performance and hazard analysis.

  4. 04

    Technology demonstrators

    Subsystem rigs, ground test vehicles and validated control experiments. Gate: repeatable test evidence.

  5. 05

    Prototype engineering

    Configuration freeze, systems integration, quality controls and test readiness. Gate: documented independent safety review.

  6. 06

    Ground and authorized flight testing

    Progressive test envelopes, instrumentation, emergency plans and regulatory permissions where needed.

  7. 07

    Certification and validation

    Prove compliance to applicable aviation and road frameworks. Timing and feasibility remain uncertain.

  8. 08

    Commercial readiness

    Only after approvals: assess production, maintenance, operations, infrastructure and customer support.

This roadmap is aspirational, not a progress report. No launch year or certification status has been established.
