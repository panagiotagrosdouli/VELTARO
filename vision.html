Our ambition

Beyond the road.
Grounded in reality.

We envision a future where personal mobility can move intelligently between roads and the sky. First, we must establish whether it can be done safely, usefully and economically.

Meet the V1 concept →
The problem

Mobility has limits.

Congested routes, geographic barriers and inflexible infrastructure motivate research into new transport systems. Flight is not inherently faster, greener or more affordable: total door-to-door outcomes must be measured.

Our thesis

One compelling experience.

The V1 concept explores whether a premium vehicle could someday combine high-quality road travel with limited, regulated airborne operation, without compromising the discipline required of aviation.

Strategic principles

Built around questions worth answering

Safety before spectacle

Technical and regulatory feasibility determine the product—not marketing imagery.

Luxury with purpose

Thoughtful design, comfort and usability should support realistic operating scenarios.

Evidence-led decisions

Use simulations, third-party reviews, customer interviews and transparent stage gates to validate the concept.

Business hypothesis

Where value could emerge

Initial research may focus on high-value private mobility, demonstration partnerships and potentially technology licensing. These are hypotheses, not established products, revenue streams or partnerships.

Customer discovery

Interview potential operators, private aviation customers and fleet stakeholders to identify useful mission profiles.

Competitive landscape

Compare conventional aircraft, premium ground vehicles and advanced air mobility solutions on total mission cost, accessibility and safety.

Commercial viability

Test capital intensity, certification cost, manufacturing feasibility, maintenance, insurance and infrastructure requirements.

VELTARO is a concept-stage initiative. No financial forecasts, market shares or product availability are asserted.
