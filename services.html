VELTARO LABS / Prospective collaboration formats

Research starts
with clarity.

Three possible formats for early-stage mobility research. These are proposed scopes for discovery conversations, not fixed-price offers or claims of currently available regulated engineering services.

01 / DISCOVERY

Mission & Problem Brief

Structure a mobility problem before spending on hardware or a full design study.

  • Stakeholder questions and mission statement
  • Constraints and assumptions register
  • Research questions and evidence gaps
02 / FEASIBILITY

Preliminary Feasibility Review

Compare first-order conceptual options using documented assumptions and published evidence.

  • High-level requirements
  • Source-traceable model assumptions
  • Key uncertainties and trade-offs
03 / EVIDENCE

Evidence & Risk Map

Identify what would need validation before a technical or commercial decision.

  • Preliminary risk and dependency list
  • Evidence strength and missing inputs
  • Prioritized next-step research plan
Working method

From question to written scope.

  1. Send a non-confidential summary of the question and intended use.
  2. Discuss feasibility, competence, deliverables, professional requirements and any confidentiality conditions.
  3. If appropriate, agree to a written scope, timeline, price and contractual terms before starting work.

Important: No flight testing, aircraft certification, engineering design approval, legal authorization, guaranteed outcome, price or delivery date is offered. Any work needing licensed professionals must be reviewed and performed by appropriately qualified persons. VELTARO V1 remains long-term concept research.
